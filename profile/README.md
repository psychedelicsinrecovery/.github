🌵 PIR® — Psychedelics In Recovery™ — is a nonprofit fellowship supporting people who integrate
psychedelic experience into their recovery from addiction — a space where 12-step principles and
psychedelic harm-reduction sit together rather than in opposition. 🙏

## 🗺️ What lives here

This organization's GitHub presence is early — most of PIR's actual work happens at
[psychedelicsinrecovery.org](https://www.psychedelicsinrecovery.org) (WordPress-hosted) and through
in-person and virtual community meetings. This space exists for the technical side of that work as it
grows: site tooling, documentation, and — eventually — any code the community needs in the open. ✨

## 🚧 In the works

A LitCom engagement portal and a TechCom helpdesk ticket system are currently being brainstormed —
something a little more cool and 🍄 psychedelic than a plain contact form. Nothing live yet, but
watch this org if it interests you.
